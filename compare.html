Common Questions

Before you decide

Which model should a first-time EV buyer pick?+
For most daily commuters doing under 40 km a day, the Spark S1 covers it comfortably and costs the least. If you want more buffer for longer rides or occasional highway stretches, the Volt X1 is our most popular pick.
Can I charge it at home on a normal socket?+
Yes — every model comes with a standard charger that works on a regular 15A home socket. We also help with wall-charger installation at no extra cost within Gorakhpur.
What does the battery warranty actually cover?+
3 years or 30,000 km, whichever comes first — covers battery capacity drop beyond the manufacturer's stated threshold. Full terms are shared at the time of purchase.
Do you offer EMI or financing?+
Yes, we work with local banks and NBFCs for on-the-spot financing. EMIs typically start around ₹2,499/month depending on the model and down payment.
Is servicing available after purchase?+
Our in-house service bay at the showroom handles routine check-ups and repairs, usually within the same visit.
